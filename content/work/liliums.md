## The problem

Liliums is a floral studio in Hyderabad. The brief was to put the bouquets online without the weight of a shop: browse, pick, message.

## What I built

A catalogue of eight collections and seventy-one bouquets, each offered as Standard, Deluxe or Premium. The site is live at [liliums.in](https://www.liliums.in).

## The hard part

Leaving the shop out. There is no checkout. Order opens WhatsApp with the bouquet already named in the message, so the customer starts the conversation with the choice already made.
